# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-minded community. Positive behavior includes constructive feedback, clear disclosure of uncertainty, respect for clinical and technical expertise, and prioritizing learner and patient safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private information, deliberate submission of patient data, and misrepresentation of educational output as clinical advice.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens people, privacy, project integrity, or safety. Reports should be made privately through `SECURITY.md`.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
