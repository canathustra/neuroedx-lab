# Data policy

## v0.1: synthetic only

NeuroEDX Lab v0.1 does not ingest, store, train on, or display patient data. Every waveform and scenario is generated from hand-authored parameters and deterministic mathematical functions.

The following are not accepted in issues, discussions, pull requests, demos, screenshots, fixtures, or documentation:

- raw EMG/NCS recordings or exports from clinical equipment;
- patient reports, referral notes, identifiers, dates, accession numbers, or metadata;
- photographs, videos, audio, or screen captures made during patient care;
- values transcribed from an identifiable or re-identifiable patient;
- copyrighted textbook pages, figures, videos, or proprietary course material.

Safe contributions include original code and diagrams, deterministic synthetic signals, fictional cases not derived from an individual, bibliographic citations, and original protocol summaries.

If sensitive data is submitted, do not quote, fork, or redistribute it. Contact the maintainers privately through `SECURITY.md` so access can be restricted and the material removed without reproducing it.

Any future proposal involving human-derived data requires separate clinical, ethical, privacy, provenance, licensing, and security review. It is outside the v0.1 scope.
