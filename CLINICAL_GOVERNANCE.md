# Clinical governance

NeuroEDX Lab teaches a measurement process; it does not provide medical advice or a diagnostic conclusion.

## Review principles

1. **Technique before interpretation.** Scenarios must surface stimulation, recording, marker, distance, and temperature pitfalls before discussing a pattern.
2. **No universal cutoffs.** A simulated threshold must be labeled pedagogic. Clinical reference values require technique-matched, laboratory-validated sources.
3. **Traceable claims.** Material clinical claims require an authoritative guideline, consensus document, systematic review, or primary study citation.
4. **Clinician review.** Changes affecting anatomy, protocol, waveform meaning, safety, or interpretation require approval from a qualified electrodiagnostic clinician.
5. **Uncertainty is visible.** The interface must distinguish technical inadequacy, a descriptive pattern, and a clinical diagnosis.
6. **No patient-derived content.** `DATA_POLICY.md` applies to every contribution and release.

Until an external clinical review is recorded, releases are marked **educational prototype**. A release label never changes the project into a medical device or authorizes clinical use.
